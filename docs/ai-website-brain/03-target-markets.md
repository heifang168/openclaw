# Target Markets

## Main Target Markets

The website should be built for overseas B2B markets.

Main target regions:

1. Middle East
2. South Africa
3. Southeast Asia
4. North America
5. South America

Each market has different customer pain points and should have specific landing pages or content sections.

## Middle East

### Typical Applications

- Oil & gas projects
- Desert construction
- Remote hotels and resorts
- Mining
- Commercial backup power
- Remote power stations

### Market Pain Points

- High temperature
- Sand and dust
- Remote project locations
- Long working hours
- Strong demand for reliable power
- Harsh outdoor conditions

### Content Direction

The website should highlight:

- Reliable power in hot climates
- Durable generator configuration
- Suitable for desert and remote projects
- Flexible gas and diesel generator options
- Industrial project support

### Suggested Message

Reliable Generator Solutions for Hot Climate and Remote Projects

## Market Page Principle

Each market page should include:

1. Market overview
2. Local power challenges
3. Recommended generator solutions
4. Application scenarios
5. Product recommendations
6. FAQ
7. Inquiry CTA

## Market CTA Direction

Use region-specific CTA messages:

- Need a generator solution for your project?
- Tell us your country, power range, voltage, and application.
- Our team will recommend a suitable configuration for your market.
