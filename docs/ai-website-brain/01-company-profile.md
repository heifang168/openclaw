# Company Profile

## Company Type

The company is a B2B supplier of industrial generator sets and related power equipment.

The company provides both gas generator sets and diesel generator sets for overseas industrial and commercial projects.

## Product Coverage

The company can provide generator solutions from small power range to large industrial power systems.

Power range includes:

- 18kVA small generators
- 30kVA to 100kVA commercial generators
- 100kVA to 500kVA industrial generators
- 500kVA to 1000kVA large generator sets
- 1000kVA+ heavy-duty power systems

The company can also sell single generator-related products when required by customers.

## Main Product Categories

- Gas Generator Sets

- Industrial Generator Sets

## Business Strength

The company may not currently compete directly with global giants such as Schneider, Cummins, or ABB in terms of brand scale.

However, the company should highlight the following strengths:

1. Flexible product configuration
2. Wide power range coverage
3. Gas and diesel generator options
4. Complete supporting power equipment
5. Multiple factory cooperation resources
6. Project-based supply capability
7. Faster response than large brands
8. More flexible customization
9. Cost-effective solution for B2B buyers
10. Suitable for overseas industrial and commercial projects

## Brand Tone

The website should present the company as a practical, reliable, and flexible industrial generator solution supplier.

The tone should be professional and trustworthy, without exaggerated claims.

## Avoided Positioning

Do not position the company as:

- The world's No.1 generator manufacturer
- A direct replacement for Cummins, Schneider, or ABB
- A fake global giant
- A low-end trading company
- A simple product reseller

## Recommended Positioning

Use this direction:

Flexible Industrial Generator Solutions for Global B2B Projects

The company should be presented as a supplier that understands project needs and can provide suitable generator configurations based on power range, fuel type, working condition, voltage, frequency, and application scenario.
