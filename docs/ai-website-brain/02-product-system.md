# Product System

## Product Strategy

The website should organize products based on B2B procurement logic.

Products should not be displayed only as a random catalog. They should be grouped by fuel type, structure type, application type, and power range.

## Main Product Families

### 1. Gas Generator Sets

Gas generator sets should be positioned for customers who need cleaner, cost-effective, and long-term continuous power solutions.

Possible fuel types include:

- Natural gas
- Biogas
- LPG
- Other available gas fuel types depending on project requirements

Typical applications:

- Industrial plants
- Oil & gas projects
- Long-term power supply
- Remote energy projects
- Commercial facilities
- Distributed energy projects

Key selling points:

- Lower operating cost potential
- Cleaner power generation
- Suitable for continuous operation
- Flexible configuration
- Project-based solution support

### 2. Diesel Generator Sets

Diesel generator sets should be positioned for backup power, emergency power, and industrial power applications.

Typical applications:

- Factory backup power
- Data center backup power
- Hospital emergency power
- Construction site power
- Mining power
- Farm and irrigation power
- Commercial backup power

Key selling points:

- Strong power output
- Fast start
- Reliable backup power
- Wide application range
- Easy fuel availability in many regions
- Suitable for harsh working conditions

### 3. Silent Generator Sets

Silent generator sets should be positioned for projects that need lower noise and better environmental adaptability.

Typical applications:

- Commercial buildings
- Hotels
- Hospitals
- Residential projects
- Events
- Urban construction sites

Key selling points:

- Lower noise
- Weather-resistant enclosure
- Integrated design
- Suitable for outdoor installation
- Better for commercial and urban projects

### 4. Open Type Generator Sets

Open type generator sets should be positioned for customers who need easier maintenance, lower initial cost, and installation inside a machine room or shelter.

Typical applications:

- Generator rooms
- Industrial plants
- Workshops
- Indoor power stations
- Customized power systems

Key selling points:

- Easy maintenance
- Cost-effective
- Flexible installation
- Suitable for indoor engineering projects

### 5. Containerized Generator Sets

Containerized generator sets should be positioned for large-scale, outdoor, mobile, or harsh-environment power projects.

Typical applications:

- Mining
- Oil & gas
- Remote construction
- Large industrial plants
- Temporary power stations
- Large backup power systems

Key selling points:

- Integrated container structure
- Better protection
- Easier transportation
- Suitable for harsh outdoor environments
- Good for high-power projects

### 6. Trailer Generator Sets

Trailer generator sets should be positioned for mobile power supply and rental power.

Typical applications:

- Temporary construction power
- Emergency power
- Events
- Rental power
- Mobile maintenance projects

Key selling points:

- Easy movement
- Flexible deployment
- Suitable for temporary power needs
- Fast on-site use

### 7. Supporting Power Equipment

The company should also present its ability to supply related power equipment, not only generator sets.

Supporting products may include:

- ATS
- Switchgear
- Control panels
- Distribution cabinets
- Base fuel tanks
- Enclosures
- Synchronizing systems
- Other electrical supporting equipment

## Power Range Presentation

The website should clearly show that the company can support a wide power range:

- 18kVA to 50kVA
- 50kVA to 100kVA
- 100kVA to 500kVA
- 500kVA to 1000kVA
- 1000kVA+

This helps different customers quickly find suitable power solutions.

## Product Page Principle

Every product page should include:

1. Hero section
2. Product overview
3. Power range
4. Key advantages
5. Typical applications
6. Configuration options
7. Technical parameters
8. FAQ
9. Inquiry CTA

## Product CTA Direction

Use clear B2B CTA phrases:

- Get a Quote
- Request Technical Data
- Talk to Our Engineer
- Send Your Power Requirement
- Get Generator Configuration
