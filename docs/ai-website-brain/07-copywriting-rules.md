# Copywriting Rules

## Language

The website should use English as the primary language.

The copy should be written for overseas B2B buyers.

## Tone

The tone should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Engineering-focused
- Buyer-oriented
- International

Avoid:

- Overly exaggerated language
- Empty marketing slogans
- Too much technical jargon without explanation
- Domestic-style sales language
- Claims that cannot be verified
- Directly attacking competitors

## Core Message

Use this core message consistently:

Gas & Diesel Generator Solutions for Global Industrial Projects

## Supporting Message

Use this supporting message when appropriate:

From 18kVA small generators to 1000kVA+ industrial power systems, we provide flexible gas and diesel generator solutions for factories, mining, construction, data centers, farms, oil & gas projects, and remote power applications.

## Brand Positioning Sentence

Use this sentence when explaining the company:

We provide flexible industrial generator solutions for global B2B projects, covering gas and diesel generator sets, supporting electrical equipment, and project-based configuration support.

## Copywriting Principle

The copy should focus on buyer problems and project needs.

Do not only say what the company sells.

Always connect products with:

1. Customer application
2. Power requirement
3. Working condition
4. Fuel type
5. Project environment
6. Inquiry action

## Recommended CTA Phrases

Use these CTA phrases:

- Get a Quote
- Request Technical Data
- Talk to Our Engineer
- Send Your Power Requirement
- Get Generator Configuration
- Find the Right Generator
- Ask for Project Support

## Inquiry Guidance Sentence

Use this sentence near forms or CTA sections:

Tell us your required power, fuel type, voltage, frequency, country, and application. Our team will recommend a suitable generator configuration for your project.

## Avoided Claims

Do not use:

- No.1 generator manufacturer
- Best generator in the world
- Better than Cummins
- Better than ABB
- Better than Schneider
- Guaranteed lowest price
- 100% perfect solution
- World leading supplier

## Better Alternatives

Use:

- Flexible generator solutions
- Project-based configuration support
- Cost-effective power systems
- Suitable for industrial and commercial projects
- Reliable backup and continuous power options
- Gas and diesel generator sets for different working conditions
- Practical solution for global B2B buyers

## Product Copy Rule

Every product page should explain:

1. What the product is
2. Who it is suitable for
3. What applications it supports
4. What power range it covers
5. What configuration options are available
6. What information the buyer should provide for quotation

## Solution Copy Rule

Every solution page should explain:

1. The industry problem
2. The recommended generator type
3. Why this solution fits the application
4. Typical power range
5. Required quotation information
6. Inquiry CTA

## Market Copy Rule

Every market page should explain:

1. Local power challenges
2. Common applications
3. Recommended generator solution
4. Procurement concerns
5. Inquiry CTA
