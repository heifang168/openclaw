## Page SEO Rule

Each important page must include:

1. SEO title
2. Meta description
3. One H1
4. Clear H2 sections
5. Natural keyword usage
6. FAQ section
7. Internal links
8. Inquiry CTA

## SEO Title Format

Recommended format:

Primary Keyword | Application or Benefit | Brand / Company

Examples:

Gas Generator Sets for Industrial Projects | Flexible Power Solutions

Diesel Generator Sets for Backup Power | Industrial Generator Supplier

Mining Power Generator Solutions | Diesel & Gas Generator Sets

## Meta Description Rule

Meta description should be clear and buyer-oriented.

Example:

Explore flexible gas and diesel generator solutions from 18kVA to 1000kVA+ for factories, mining, construction, data centers, farms, and remote power projects. Send your power requirement for a suitable configuration.

## H1 Rule

Each page should have only one H1.

The H1 should clearly include the main page keyword.

Examples:

Gas Generator Sets for Industrial Power Projects

Diesel Generator Sets for Reliable Backup Power

Mining Power Generator Solutions

## FAQ SEO Rule

Every product, solution, and market page should include 4 to 6 FAQs.

FAQ topics should answer real buyer questions:

- What power range do you provide?
- Can you provide both gas and diesel generator sets?
- What information is needed for a quotation?
- Can the generator be customized for voltage and frequency?
- Is this solution suitable for harsh environments?
- Do you provide supporting electrical equipment?
