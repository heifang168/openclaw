# Website Positioning

## Core Positioning

Gas & Diesel Generator Solutions for Global Industrial Projects

The website should position the company as a flexible B2B supplier of gas and diesel generator solutions for overseas industrial and commercial projects.

## What the Website Should Communicate

The website should clearly communicate:

1. The company provides both gas and diesel generator sets.
2. The company covers power ranges from 18kVA to 1000kVA+.
3. The company can support different applications and industries.
4. The company provides supporting electrical equipment and project configuration support.
5. The company is suitable for global B2B project buyers.
6. The company offers flexible, cost-effective, and responsive generator solutions.

## Differentiation Strategy

The company should not directly compete with global giants based on brand scale.

Instead, the website should differentiate through:

- Flexibility
- Configuration capability
- Faster communication
- Wider project adaptability
- Cost-effective supply
- Complete generator and electrical equipment support
- Ability to serve different power ranges
- Gas and diesel options
- Practical B2B project support

## Positioning Against Global Brands

Do not use exaggerated claims such as:

- World leading generator manufacturer
- No.1 power solution provider
- The best generator supplier in the world
- Better than Cummins
- Better than ABB
- Better than Schneider

Use practical positioning instead:

- Flexible generator solutions for industrial projects
- Cost-effective power systems for global B2B buyers
- Gas and diesel generator sets for factories, mining, construction, and remote sites
- From small generators to 1000kVA+ industrial power systems
- Project-based generator configuration support

## Website Tone

The website tone should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Engineering-focused
- Buyer-oriented
- International

Avoid tone that is:

- Overly exaggerated
- Too domestic
- Too casual
- Too technical without explanation
- Too salesy
- Too generic

## Core Brand Message

Flexible Generator Solutions for Global B2B Power Projects

## Homepage Hero Message

Headline:

Gas & Diesel Generator Solutions for Global Industrial Projects

Subheadline:

From 18kVA small generators to 1000kVA+ industrial power systems, we provide flexible gas and diesel generator solutions for factories, mining, construction, data centers, farms, oil & gas projects, and remote power applications.

CTA Buttons:

- Get a Quote
- View Products

## Trust Building Direction

The website should build trust through:

- Clear product categories
- Real factory images
- Technical specifications
- Application scenarios
- Market-specific content
- Project cases
- Certificates if available
- Testing process
- Packing and delivery process
- FAQ
- Easy inquiry form

## Conversion Direction

Every important page should guide the visitor to send an inquiry.

The website should encourage customers to provide:

- Required power
- Fuel type
- Voltage
- Frequency
- Country
- Application
- Contact information

## Final Website Goal

The final website should help overseas B2B buyers quickly understand the company, find suitable generator solutions, build trust, and send an inquiry.
